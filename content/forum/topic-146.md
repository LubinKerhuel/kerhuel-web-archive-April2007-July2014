<div class="forum-post-body">Apologies: it was my mistake.  I can't work out exactly what I was doing earlier, but the<br>
<br>

<br>
I mistook this for "slave data out" instead of "SPI data out".  It all works correctly<br>
<br>
now.<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>
On another note, two small corrections to the English on the blocks:<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>
"Block Ordering Input / Ouptut" should be "Block Ordering Input / Output" everywhere<br>
<br>
The SPI Configuration block specifically has<br>
<br>
"Bus Configuratin Time" which should be "Bus Configuration Time" .<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>
"output change on transition" should probably be "Output Change on Transition"<br>
<br>
(all other field headings use capital letters).<br>
