<h3>Re: Problem using ADC and SPI</h3>
<div class="forum-post-body">Hello All,<br>
<br>
 From what I read seems that your SPI is an error mode. If you get an overwrite error, your dsPIC will continue working normally but your SPI engine will freeze until you aknowledge the error and clear the error flag. It is a good idea enable the SPI error interrupt and handle the interrupt accordingly in an ISR. Make sure you read the 33F family reference manual to see how to go about it.<br>
<br>
<br>
<br>
Hope this helps.</div>
